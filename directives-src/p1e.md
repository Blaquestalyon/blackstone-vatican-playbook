### WBS: P1.E.1.1
:: SUMMARY
Draft the one-page strategy teaser copy. The short document that opens doors before the full package.
:: DIRECTIVE
The teaser is the first thing a gatekeeper or committee sees. It has to earn the second meeting.

**Actions**
1. Write one page that states the strategy, the values-screening differentiator, the performance headline (relative to benchmark), and why this fund fits a Catholic/faith-based mandate.
2. Lead with the proof: GIPS-verified track record, independent audit, recognized custodian and administrator. This is the credibility the whole program was built to establish.
3. Keep every claim consistent with the ADV, the methodology, and the audited numbers, and keep it compliant (no performance claim the record does not support).

**Deliverable:** approved one-page teaser copy.

**Done when:** the copy is drafted, accurate, and ready for design.

### WBS: P1.E.1.2
:: SUMMARY
Design and format the teaser into a polished, on-brand one-pager.
:: DIRECTIVE
**Actions**
1. Lay out the approved copy into a clean, institutional one-pager with clear hierarchy and space for the key performance and proof-point figures.
2. Include required disclosures and the GIPS compliance statement where performance is shown.
3. Produce a print-ready and a shareable digital version.

**Deliverable:** a designed, disclosure-complete teaser in final formats.

**Done when:** the teaser is production-ready.

### WBS: P1.E.2.1
:: SUMMARY
Assemble the full manager package: teaser, DDQ, GIPS-verified track, audited financials, and ADV. This is the M8 proof-stack milestone the whole channel strategy waits on.
:: DIRECTIVE
**CRITICAL PATH — M8 proof-stack milestone.** This package is the deliverable Phase 1 exists to produce. It is what gets submitted to every gatekeeper (Phase 2), channel (Phase 4), and the Vatican (Phase 5).

**Actions**
1. Assemble the complete package: teaser, master DDQ, GIPS verification report and compliant presentation, audited financials, Form ADV, and the screening methodology.
2. Verify internal consistency across every document: strategy, fees, performance, and screening claims must match exactly. Cross-document contradictions are what diligence teams catch.
3. Confirm every referenced proof (GIPS verification, audit) is final and in hand, not pending.
4. Organize it into a clean, navigable data-room structure.

**Deliverable:** the complete M8 manager package in a diligence-ready data room.

**Done when:** every component is final, consistent, and assembled, gated only by compliance review (P1.E.2.2) and Kate's approval (P1.E.2.3).

### WBS: P1.E.2.2
:: SUMMARY
Compliance review of all marketing claims in the package. Make sure every claim is supportable and compliant before it goes out.
:: DIRECTIVE
**CRITICAL PATH.** One unsupportable claim can sink credibility with an institutional buyer. Review hard.

**Actions**
1. Have compliance (and counsel where needed) review every performance and marketing claim against the audited and GIPS-verified record and the marketing rule.
2. Check required disclosures, benchmark descriptions, and the GIPS compliance statement are present and correct.
3. Log and fix any issue; re-review until clean.

**Deliverable:** a compliance-cleared package with a documented review record.

**Done when:** compliance signs off that every claim is supportable and compliant.

Kate approves the marketing claims and signs off on the final package. Principal approval to release the M8 proof stack.

### WBS: P1.C.1.1
:: SUMMARY
Draft the custodian RFP and shortlist qualified custodians. The custodian holds the assets; institutional buyers require a credible one.
:: DIRECTIVE
A recognized, independent custodian is table stakes for institutional and Vatican capital. Run a real selection process, not a handshake.

**Actions**
1. Draft an RFP covering: asset types held, safekeeping and segregation, reporting and data feeds to the administrator, pricing, technology/OMS integration, minimums, and experience with values-screened SMAs.
2. Identify custodians that clear institutional diligence and can service an SMA program at the intended scale.
3. Issue the RFP to a shortlist and set a response deadline.

**Deliverable:** an issued RFP and a shortlist of qualified custodians.

**Done when:** the RFP is out and a defensible shortlist is assembled.

### WBS: P1.C.1.2
:: SUMMARY
Run diligence and fee negotiation with the custodian finalists. Pick the right custodian on service and cost.
:: DIRECTIVE
Compare finalists on substance, then negotiate.

**Actions**
1. Score RFP responses against weighted criteria (financial strength, service model, integration, reporting quality, price, references).
2. Take references, especially from other values-screened or emerging managers.
3. Negotiate the fee schedule and service levels; get pricing in writing tied to defined deliverables.
4. Recommend a finalist with a short rationale memo.

**Deliverable:** a scored comparison and a recommended custodian with negotiated terms.

**Done when:** a custodian is selected and terms are agreed pending signature.

### WBS: P1.C.1.3

Execute the onboarding cleanly so it does not become the bottleneck for account activation (P1.A.2).

2. Establish reporting and data feeds from custodian to administrator and OMS.
3. Confirm account structure, cash movement, and settlement instructions.

**Deliverable:** completed onboarding and an opened custody account awaiting signature.

**Done when:** onboarding is complete and the account is ready for Kate's signature at P1.C.1.4.

### WBS: P1.C.4.1
:: SUMMARY
Define the firm and composites per GIPS standards. This starts the GIPS-compliant track record that the entire critical path runs through.
:: DIRECTIVE
**CRITICAL PATH.** GIPS (Global Investment Performance Standards) compliance is the spine of the whole timeline: it feeds verification (C5), the M8 proof-stack package, and ultimately the Vatican allocation. Do this precisely; errors here ripple everywhere.

**Actions**
1. Define the GIPS "firm" (the entity being held out to prospective clients) exactly per the standard.
2. Define composites: group all fee-paying, discretionary portfolios managed to the values-screened strategy into the appropriate composite(s), with clear inclusion/exclusion criteria and defined discretion.
3. Document composite definitions, the creation date, and the inception date.
4. Confirm the definitions with the future verifier's expectations in mind to avoid rework.

**Deliverable:** documented firm definition and composite construction.

**Done when:** the firm and composites are defined to GIPS standard and ready to receive the historical input set (P1.C.4.2).

### WBS: P1.C.4.2
:: SUMMARY
Compile the complete historical performance and account records into the GIPS input set. Fund-only data; the record's completeness is non-negotiable.
:: DIRECTIVE
**CRITICAL PATH. Kate only — DATA.** GIPS requires the complete history with no cherry-picking; only the fund holds these records and only Kate can attest they are complete.

**Actions**
1. Assemble every relevant portfolio's full history for the composite: returns, holdings, cash flows, and fees, back to inception.
2. Ensure completeness: GIPS requires all fee-paying discretionary portfolios in the composite for all periods. Excluding a weak account is a compliance failure, not an option.
3. Reconcile the input set against custody and administrator records so the numbers tie out.
4. Structure the data in the format the return calculation (P1.C.4.3) needs.

**Deliverable:** a complete, reconciled GIPS input data set.

**Done when:** the full history is compiled, reconciled, and ready for compliant return calculation.

> Completeness is the thing verifiers test hardest. A missing account discovered later can invalidate the composite and reset this part of the critical path.

### WBS: P1.C.4.3
:: SUMMARY
Calculate GIPS-compliant composite returns and the required statistics.
:: DIRECTIVE
**CRITICAL PATH.** Compute the numbers exactly as GIPS prescribes.

**Actions**
1. Calculate time-weighted composite returns per GIPS methodology, with proper treatment of cash flows and asset-weighting across portfolios.
2. Compute all required statistics: composite and benchmark returns, number of portfolios, composite assets, total firm assets, dispersion, and the three-year annualized standard deviation once history allows.
3. Reconcile results against the audited financials (P1.C.3) and the administrator's numbers; discrepancies must be resolved, not smoothed.

**Deliverable:** GIPS-compliant composite returns and statistics.

**Done when:** returns and required statistics are calculated, reconciled, and ready for the presentation and P&P manual (P1.C.4.4).

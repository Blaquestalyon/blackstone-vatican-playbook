### WBS: P3.L.1.1
:: SUMMARY
Map the target Investment Committee and the networks surrounding it. Know the people who ultimately influence the allocation.
:: DIRECTIVE
Phase 3 is relationship groundwork into the investment-committee layer that surrounds the Vatican decision. Start with a clear map.

**Actions**
1. Map the target Investment Committee: members, roles, terms, and how the committee makes manager decisions.
2. Map the surrounding networks: advisors, consultants, and peers who influence or connect to committee members.
3. Identify the connection paths from the fund's existing network to these people.

**Deliverable:** an IC-and-network map with candidate connection paths.

**Done when:** the committee and its influence network are mapped with routes in identified.

### WBS: P3.L.1.2
:: SUMMARY
Identify current officeholders and re-verify them against primary sources.
:: DIRECTIVE
Committee rosters change. Reaching a former member, or misnaming a current one, damages credibility.

**Actions**
1. Identify the current holder of each relevant committee and network role.
2. Re-verify each against a primary source (official listings, the institution's own materials, direct confirmation) and record the date and source.
3. Flag anyone you cannot confirm and resolve before any outreach.

**Deliverable:** a verified, sourced, dated list of current officeholders.

**Done when:** every name you intend to use is confirmed current and sourced.

> Confirm-before-use discipline: named officeholders in this campaign must be re-verified against primary sources immediately before outreach, not relied on from an earlier map.

### WBS: P3.L.2.1
:: SUMMARY
Maintain a re-verification cadence for named officeholders through the campaign. A standing action, on the critical path.
:: DIRECTIVE
**STANDING ACTION across the campaign — critical path.** Names verified once will drift. Keep them current continuously.

**Actions**
1. Set a recurring re-verification cadence (for example monthly, and always immediately before a named outreach or delivery).
2. Re-check each key officeholder against primary sources and update the master contact record with dates.
3. Trigger an alert whenever a change is detected so dependent outreach can be adjusted.

**Deliverable:** a continuously maintained, dated officeholder register.

**Done when:** the register is live and the cadence is running. This item stays active for the duration of the campaign rather than closing once.

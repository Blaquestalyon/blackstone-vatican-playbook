### WBS: P2.G.1.1
:: SUMMARY
Draft the master DDQ answering the standard institutional questions. One authoritative due-diligence questionnaire you reuse and tailor everywhere.
:: DIRECTIVE
The DDQ is the workhorse document of institutional fundraising. Build one strong master version so every gatekeeper and the Vatican start from the same vetted base.

**Actions**
1. Draft answers to the standard institutional DDQ set: firm and ownership, team and key-person background, strategy and process, the values-screening methodology, risk management, performance, operations, custody/administration/audit, compliance and regulatory, and business continuity.
2. Write to institutional standard: specific, evidenced, and consistent with the ADV, methodology, and audited/GIPS record.
3. Structure it so sections can be lifted into gatekeeper-specific templates without rewriting the substance.

**Deliverable:** a complete master DDQ.

**Done when:** the master DDQ answers the standard questions accurately and is ready for exhibits and attestation.
